# Dashboard

* Be inspired by the feel of android auto.
* When no notifications, the music player should fill the available space with cover art.
* Section for speed should show speed limit when available.
* When unread notifications are available, they should be interactive. Like replying or dismissing.
* When replying, keep in mind that we are running a motorcycle. Preferrably the reply should be spoken to the phone app. And the table should show the reply as a spoken message before it is sent. Again, think android auto.
* I am not sure if letting the 1/3 space usage of screen is optimal. You can reduce it if you have knowledge of more optimal space usage.
* You are allowed to do your best to make it look better if you have a sense of design.
